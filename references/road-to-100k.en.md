# Road to 100,000 followers

The target is a real account total **above 100,000 followers**. The checkpoints are **250, 1,000, 10,000, and 100,001**. They are review points, not growth promises, and do not replace the goal of acquiring more high-quality blue-verified followers. Recheck the latest total and quality evidence at each checkpoint; crossing a number once does not establish lasting progress.

## Start with the evidence gaps

Use the latest timestamped observations in [public metrics](../public/metrics.json). Historical rows are dated baselines, not current account counts. Total follower changes do not establish RISE's causal effect or identify newly acquired blue-verified followers.

Verified-list observations may include existing followers gaining verification. A list without recorded badge colors is not a blue-follower count. Confirm new identities through complete before/after snapshots; otherwise report observed arrivals and the remaining ambiguity.

Classify quality separately using the [measurement rubric](measurement.md): audience relevance, original content, and substantive discussion, supported by public evidence. Keep unreviewed accounts `unknown`, include them in the denominator, and report the possible quality-share range. Rising totals must not hide missing quality or conversion evidence.

## Change one testable variable per round

1. **Record a real baseline.** Save exact timestamps, follower totals, visible counters, snapshot coverage, and whether engagement came from the account owner or independent users. Do not reconstruct missing historical lists.
2. **Choose one variable.** Test an opening, topic entry point, format, or relevant discussion audience. Write the hypothesis, conditions to hold similar, action budget, and observation windows before acting.
3. **Provide useful content.** Publish an original method, case, resource, or work sample that stands on its own. Answer concrete questions in relevant discussions and respond to substantive feedback. Use the skill's adjustable budget; avoid repeated notifications, mechanical follow exchanges, and repetitive promotion. Verify that actions persist.
4. **Observe at matching post ages.** Record publication time and review around 60 minutes and 24 hours. Log actual age if late. Do not compare an older post's lifetime views with a younger post when matching-age observations are missing. Track pending reviews explicitly.
5. **Review before expanding.** Compare independent substantive replies, impressions to profile visits, confirmed new blue followers, high-quality counts, quality share, and unknown counts. Collect multiple comparable content observations before repeating a promising approach. Small samples and concurrent account activity remain limitations.

Impressions, public views, independent engagement, and follows are separate evidence. Owner replies, likes, reposts, and pinned-thread updates are not independent audience engagement. An external like does not prove a new follower. Record unavailable metrics as `null`; use zero only when visibly measured. Never divide total follower net growth by post views and call it conversion. Follow conversion requires visible attributed follows and profile visits from the same window; a zero denominator leaves the rate undefined.

## Checkpoints

| Follower total | Priority | Evidence before expanding effort |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **250** | Finish outstanding quality reviews, distinguish owner actions from external feedback, and complete pending 24-hour content observations. Test a concrete problem in AI tools, independent development, or product building. | A verified latest total, clearly marked gaps, and at least one complete content-age observation pair that informs the next change. |
| **1,000** | Collect 60-minute and 24-hour observations for at least three original posts on a consistent content line. Retain failures. Change one variable at a time and continue identity and quality checks. | Useful external feedback across multiple samples, with interpretable profile-visit or follow evidence. Report quality and unknown counts together; keep batches small when evidence is insufficient. |
| **10,000** | Develop repeatedly useful material into case series, reusable resources, or work updates. Choose the next topic from real feedback. Build relevant relationships and mutually agreed collaborations. | New high-quality followers across multiple completed windows, with no clear deterioration in audience fit as reach expands. Document quality-sample coverage; one viral post is insufficient. |
| **100,001** | Verify totals, departures, blue identity, and quality evidence. Maintain useful content and resources; write validated lessons and failure conditions back into the skill. | A fresh observation confirms the total exceeds 100,000. Report the total-follower goal separately from blue and high-quality acquisition results. |

Sample counts are adjustable working budgets, not X ranking thresholds or statistical significance requirements. Crossing a checkpoint does not authorize purchased followers, unsolicited messages, or indiscriminate bulk actions.

## Change the variable at the observed break

- **Matching-age impressions do not improve:** verify saved actions, specific content value, and audience fit. Test one topic entry point or opening; do not infer throttling without evidence.
- **Impressions exist but profile visits are zero:** test a clearer connection to the account's continuing value, such as a useful resource or follow-up case. A small zero-visit sample does not establish the cause.
- **Profile visits exist but attributed follows are zero:** inspect consistency between content promises and the public profile, and test one change within existing authorization. Unavailable attributed follows remain unknown.
- **Volume rises but high-quality counts do not:** finish unknown reviews first. If mismatch is demonstrated, change the discussion audience or original case. Keep the rubric and denominator intact.
- **Actions revert or explicit restrictions appear:** stop that action type, record it, and continue permitted independent work without circumvention.

## Recursive updates and timing

Update GitHub and the original pinned thread only for real new resources, completed observations, or actual skill improvements, following the [pinned-update plan](pinned-updates.md). Use real permalinks and dated counters; mutual references do not prove causality.

**The ETA is unknown.** Do not extrapolate short-window growth into a date or promise exponential gains. Unattended X operation has not been configured. Do not claim offline monitoring or scheduled reviews without an actually available, configured scheduler. Each review should report completed actions, measured results, unknowns, and the next observation time.
